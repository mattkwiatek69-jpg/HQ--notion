# Primal Health Hub

A personal health library and planning workspace: supplied knowledge, the owner's own notes, a weekly plan, and a record of what actually happened.

## Planning

**Routine**:
A named sequence of exercises with its own steps, length and equipment; the definition, independent of when it is done.
_Avoid_: Session, workout (except the gym routines named Workout 1–3)

**Plan**:
The weekly template saying which routines sit in which part of which weekday, and for how many minutes.
_Avoid_: Schedule, timetable (for the template itself)

**Part of the day**:
One of morning, training, evening or before bed; the slots a routine is placed into.
_Avoid_: Block, slot (in prose)

**Daily habit**:
A fixed item that runs in a part of the day every day (or on named weekdays) without being a placed routine.
_Avoid_: Task, check-in

**Occurrence**:
One planned instance of a routine or daily habit on one calendar date, derived from the Plan; it exists whether or not anything is logged against it.
_Avoid_: Session, entry

**Log event**:
A record that the owner started, did or skipped one Occurrence, with the time it was recorded and a snapshot of what was done.
_Avoid_: Tick, completion, check

**Missed**:
An Occurrence whose time has passed with no Log event; a fact about the record, not a judgment.
_Avoid_: Failed, overdue

## Guidance

**Guide**:
The per-routine statement of when, how often and in what order a routine fits, each part sourced to the OP docs or marked as a Suggestion.

**Suggestion**:
Guidance written where the OP docs are silent; never the owner's and never presented as the docs.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, advice

**Preview**:
The exact before-and-after Plan a Suggestion would produce, shown for confirmation before anything changes.

## Knowledge

**Topic**:
A Library page of supplied guidance on one subject, grouped under a Library group.
_Avoid_: System, extra (legacy section names)

**Note**:
A piece of the owner's captured thinking: an observation, question, quote, or a do or don't; filed under a Topic or waiting in the Inbox.
_Avoid_: Entry, memo

**Inbox**:
The place Notes wait until they are filed under a Topic or archived.

**Source**:
Where a piece of information came from: the OP docs, the owner's notes, a named person or document, or a Suggestion.
_Avoid_: Reference, citation (for the attribution itself)

**Override**:
The owner's personal change to a supplied record's title, summary or note, layered over the original, which is kept.
_Avoid_: Edit (when the original is meant), customisation

**Collection**:
A named list of references to records; it points, never copies.
_Avoid_: Folder, list
